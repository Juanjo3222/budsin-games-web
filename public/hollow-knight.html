About Hollow Knight

Hollow Knight is a critically acclaimed action-adventure game set in the hauntingly beautiful world of Hallownest. As the Knight, you explore vast interconnected environments, battle challenging bosses, and uncover the secrets of a fallen insect kingdom. This browser version brings the atmospheric experience directly to your browser.

How to Play

Explore the interconnected world of Hallownest, fighting enemies and discovering new abilities that let you access previously unreachable areas. Defeat bosses to earn powerful upgrades. Talk to NPCs to learn about the lore and find hidden paths. Use your map and compass to navigate the vast world.

Controls

  • WASD / Arrow Keys: Move your character
  • Z / J: Attack with your nail
  • X / K: Focus SOUL to heal
  • C / L: Dash
  • Up + Attack: Strike upward
  • Down + Attack: Strike downward (pogo on enemies)

Tips

  • Explore everywhere — many secrets are hidden off the main path
  • Heal safely during boss fights when there's a brief pause
  • Use your map and buy pins to track important locations
  • Upgrade your nail as soon as you can afford it
  • Learn boss attack patterns to find openings
