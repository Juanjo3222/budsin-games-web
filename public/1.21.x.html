Minecraft 1.21.x

Minecraft 1.21.x (Eaglercraft) lets you play the latest version of Minecraft directly in your browser. Explore infinite procedurally generated worlds, build amazing structures, craft tools and weapons, and survive against mobs. This browser client brings the full Minecraft experience with no download required.

How to Play

Once loaded, create a new single-player world or join a multiplayer server. Use the classic Minecraft controls to move, build, and interact with the world. Gather resources during the day, build shelter before nightfall, and explore caves for valuable minerals.

Controls

  • WASD: Move
  • Space: Jump
  • Left Click: Mine / attack
  • Right Click: Place block / interact
  • E: Open inventory
  • 1-9: Hotbar selection
  • Shift: Sneak

Tips

  • Start by punching trees to collect wood for basic tools
  • Build a crafting table to access more recipes
  • Create a shelter before the first night to avoid monsters
  • Find iron ore in caves to make better tools and armor
