Odd Bot Out

Odd Bot Out is a charming puzzle game where you play as a robot who must escape each level by reaching the exit. Use your unique abilities to manipulate blocks, activate switches, and navigate through cleverly designed rooms. Each puzzle requires creative thinking and careful planning.

How to Play

Guide the robot to the exit in each level. Push blocks, pull levers, and activate mechanisms to create a path. Some puzzles require specific sequences or timing. Think logically and experiment with different approaches when stuck.

Controls

  • Arrow Keys / WASD: Move the robot
  • Space: Interact with objects
  • Z: Undo last move
  • R: Reset the level

Tips

  • Observe the level layout before making your first move
  • Use the undo feature to backtrack without restarting
  • Sometimes the solution requires an unconventional approach
  • If stuck, take a break and come back with fresh eyes
