Super Smash Bros

Super Smash Bros is the ultimate Nintendo crossover fighting game. Choose your fighter from iconic Nintendo characters like Mario, Link, Pikachu, and Donkey Kong. Battle on dynamic stages and knock your opponents off the screen. The more damage you deal, the farther they fly.

How to Play

Fight against other players or AI in free-for-all battles. The goal is to knock opponents off the stage. Each hit increases damage percentage, making characters fly farther. Use special attacks, items, and stage elements to gain the advantage. The last one standing wins.

Controls

  • WASD / Arrow Keys: Move and jump
  • Z: Attack
  • X: Special attack
  • C: Shield / block
  • V: Grab

Tips

  • Learn each character's special moves — they vary widely
  • Use shields to block incoming attacks
  • Smash attacks (direction + attack) deal more knockback
  • Collect items that appear on stage for powerful attacks
