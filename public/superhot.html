SUPERHOT

SUPERHOT is the groundbreaking first-person shooter where time moves only when you move. Enemies are red, you are white, and bullets are slow. Plan your moves carefully, take out enemies with precision, and feel like a genius in this minimalist action puzzle game.

How to Play

Move through each level and eliminate all red enemies. Time slows to a crawl when you stand still, giving you time to plan. Use weapons, throw objects, and use melee attacks. Dodge bullets by moving out of their path. Survive each encounter to progress.

Controls

  • WASD: Move
  • Mouse: Look around / aim
  • Left Click: Shoot / throw
  • Right Click: Melee attack / grab
  • R: Reload / switch weapon

Tips

  • Use the time-slow mechanic to plan your next moves
  • Throw empty guns at enemies for instant kills
  • Dodge bullets by sidestepping — they move slowly
  • Pick up enemy weapons by moving near them
