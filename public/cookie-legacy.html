About Cookie Clicker Legacy

Cookie Clicker Legacy is the classic idle clicker game where your goal is to bake as many cookies as possible. Start by clicking the giant cookie, then use your cookies to purchase upgrades, buildings, and special abilities that automate cookie production. The legacy edition includes classic features and content from the original game.

How to Play

Click the giant cookie to earn cookies. Spend cookies on upgrades like cursors, grandmas, farms, and factories that automatically produce cookies over time. Unlock achievements, discover hidden features, and keep expanding your cookie empire. The game runs even when you're away, making it a true idle experience.

Controls

  • Mouse Click: Click the giant cookie to bake cookies manually
  • Left Click: Purchase upgrades and buildings from the shop
  • Golden Cookies: Click them when they appear for temporary bonuses

Tips

  • Buy upgrades early — they make a big difference in cookie production
  • Focus on the highest-producing buildings first
  • Click golden cookies for valuable time-limited bonuses
  • Prestige (ascend) after your first playthrough for permanent upgrades
  • Let the game run idle — production continues while you're away
