Bitcoin Clicker

Bitcoin Clicker is an addictive idle clicker game where you build your virtual cryptocurrency empire. Start by clicking to mine bitcoins, then invest your earnings in upgrades that automate mining and increase your hash rate. Watch your digital fortune grow as you unlock new technologies and achievements.

How to Play

Click the bitcoin to mine it manually. Use your mined bitcoins to purchase miners, upgrades, and boosters that automatically generate more coins over time. Unlock new tiers of technology to exponentially increase your production. The game continues running even when you're away.

Controls

  • Mouse Click: Click the bitcoin to mine
  • Click upgrades: Purchase from the shop panel

Tips

  • Buy the cheapest upgrades first for steady growth
  • Focus on automated miners to earn while idle
  • Save up for multiplier upgrades for exponential gains
  • Come back regularly to collect your idle earnings and reinvest
