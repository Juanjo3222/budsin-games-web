Centipede

Centipede is a classic arcade game originally released by Atari in 1981. Shoot at the centipede as it makes its way down through a field of mushrooms. Each segment you hit turns into a mushroom, making the path more complex. A timeless classic that defined a generation of arcade gaming.

How to Play

Move your shooter at the bottom of the screen. Shoot at the centipede segments as they descend through the mushroom field. Each hit turns a segment into a mushroom. Avoid the centipede, spiders, fleas, and scorpions. Clear levels by eliminating all centipede segments.

Controls

  • Left/Right Arrows: Move horizontally
  • Up/Down Arrows: Move vertically
  • Space / Z: Shoot

Tips

  • Shoot the centipede multiple times to break it into smaller segments
  • Clear a path through mushrooms before the centipede reaches you
  • Watch out for spiders that bounce around randomly
  • Use the narrow passages to your advantage
