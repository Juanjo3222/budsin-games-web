Galaga

Galaga is a classic arcade shoot-'em-up from the 1980s. Pilot your starfighter against waves of alien insects in this timeless space shooter. Destroy all enemies in each wave while avoiding their dive-bomb attacks. The iconic arcade experience, now in your browser.

How to Play

Move your ship left and right at the bottom of the screen. Shoot upward at the alien formations. Aliens will dive down to attack you — dodge them and shoot them down. Dual fighters can be captured by aliens; rescue them to double your firepower.

Controls

  • Left/Right Arrows: Move your ship
  • Space / Z: Fire lasers

Tips

  • Stay near the center for better coverage of the screen
  • Let aliens come to you before firing for better accuracy
  • Rescue captured ships for double firepower (dual fighter bonus)
  • Learn the formation patterns to predict dive attacks
