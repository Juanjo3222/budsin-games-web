Super Mario World

Super Mario World is the iconic SNES platformer that defined a generation. Join Mario on his quest through Dinosaur Land to rescue Princess Peach from Bowser. Ride Yoshi, discover secret exits, and explore multiple paths through vibrant levels. The definitive Mario adventure.

How to Play

Guide Mario through each level, jumping on enemies and avoiding hazards. Reach the giant goal tape at the end of each level. Collect coins, power-ups, and Yoshi eggs along the way. Find secret exits to unlock alternate paths on the world map.

Controls

  • Left/Right Arrows: Move Mario
  • Space / Z: Jump
  • X / Hold Space: Run / Spin jump
  • Down: Crouch / Enter doors

Tips

  • Find secret exits to discover new paths and areas
  • Use Yoshi's tongue to eat enemies and gain special abilities
  • Collect 100 coins for an extra life
  • Look for hidden blocks and passages
