About Rocket Goal

Rocket Goal is an exciting rocket-powered car soccer game. Drive your rocket car across the field, hit the giant ball, and score goals against your opponent. With simple controls and fast-paced matches, it's easy to pick up but hard to master. Play against the AI or challenge a friend.

How to Play

Control your rocket-powered car on the field. Your goal is to hit the ball into the opponent's goal while defending your own. Use your rocket boost to gain speed and aerial control. Matches are timed — the player with the most goals at the end wins.

Controls

  • WASD / Arrow Keys: Drive your car around the field
  • Space: Activate rocket boost for speed burst
  • Shift: Jump / flip in the air

Tips

  • Use boost strategically — don't waste it all at once
  • Learn to aerial hit the ball for unpredictable angles
  • Defend your goal when the opponent has the ball
  • Use walls to bounce the ball into tricky positions
  • Practice ball control to set up scoring opportunities
