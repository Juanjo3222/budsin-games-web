About Dumb Ways to Die

Dumb Ways to Die is a quirky and darkly humorous mini-game collection based on the viral safety campaign. Test your reflexes across dozens of absurd scenarios where one wrong move leads to a hilarious demise. Poke, prod, swipe, and tap your way through increasingly challenging levels.

How to Play

Each mini-game presents a different situation where you must avoid dying in a dumb way. Follow the on-screen instructions carefully — you might need to poke, swipe, tap, or drag objects. Complete all mini-games in a round to advance. The game gets faster and more challenging as you progress.

Controls

  • Mouse: Click, drag, and interact with objects on screen
  • Follow on-screen prompts: Each mini-game has unique controls

Tips

  • Read the instructions carefully before each mini-game
  • React quickly — many challenges are timed
  • Practice each mini-game to memorize the pattern
  • Stay calm under pressure — panic leads to mistakes
  • Have fun with it — the humor is part of the experience
