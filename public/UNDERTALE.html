UNDERTALE

UNDERTALE is a groundbreaking RPG where you can choose to fight or spare every enemy you encounter. Fall into the underground world of monsters and navigate through a deeply emotional story. Your choices affect the characters, the world, and the ending. A unique game where kindness is a valid strategy.

How to Play

Explore the underground world, talk to characters, and solve puzzles. When you encounter enemies, you can fight them or use ACT options to spare them. Dodge attacks in bullet-hell style combat sequences. Every choice matters — the story changes based on your actions.

Controls

  • Arrow Keys: Move / navigate menus
  • Z / Enter: Confirm / interact
  • X / Shift: Cancel / back
  • C: Open menu

Tips

  • Try sparing enemies — there's always a peaceful option
  • Talk to every character for story and hints
  • Explore thoroughly to find hidden items and secrets
  • Your choices have lasting consequences on the story
